# Infusion Pump Biomedical Engineering Presentation

## Part 3 – Slides 21–30

### Slide 21 – Calibration
Calibration ensures the displayed and delivered flow rate matches the programmed value.
- Use a certified Infusion Device Analyzer.
- Test multiple flow rates (e.g., 1, 25, 100 mL/h).
- Record measured error.
- Adjust per manufacturer service manual if out of tolerance.

---

### Slide 22 – Electrical Safety Testing
Tests:
- Protective earth resistance
- Earth leakage current
- Enclosure leakage current
- Patient leakage current (if applicable)
- Functional inspection

Reference concept: IEC 60601 electrical safety.

---

### Slide 23 – Performance Verification
Verify:
- Flow accuracy
- Volume accuracy
- Occlusion detection
- Air-in-line detection
- Battery runtime
- Alarm volume
- Display and keypad

---

### Slide 24 – Troubleshooting Workflow

1. Verify user complaint.
2. Perform visual inspection.
3. Check AC power and battery.
4. Review error/alarm code.
5. Test sensors.
6. Verify flow accuracy.
7. Repair or replace faulty parts.
8. Repeat functional and safety tests.
9. Document the repair.

---

### Slide 25 – Occlusion Alarm
Possible causes:
- Kinked IV tubing
- Closed clamp
- Blocked catheter
- Faulty pressure sensor

Corrective actions:
- Inspect tubing
- Remove obstruction
- Test pressure sensor
- Verify alarm operation

---

### Slide 26 – Air-in-Line Alarm
Causes:
- Air bubbles in tubing
- Improper priming
- Dirty optical sensor

Corrective actions:
- Re-prime tubing
- Clean sensor
- Replace IV set if necessary

---

### Slide 27 – Battery and Power System
Maintenance:
- Inspect battery health
- Check charging circuit
- Replace aged batteries
- Verify runtime under load
- Inspect AC inlet and power cord

---

### Slide 28 – Common Replaceable Parts
- Rechargeable battery
- Keypad
- LCD display
- Power supply module
- Motor
- Pressure sensor
- Air sensor
- Door latch
- Fuses

Always use manufacturer-approved spare parts.

---

### Slide 29 – Risk Management
Potential hazards:
- Over-infusion
- Under-infusion
- Air embolism
- Electrical shock
- Battery failure

Risk reduction:
- Preventive maintenance
- Calibration
- Alarm verification
- User training
- Proper documentation

---

### Slide 30 – Case Study
Scenario:
An ICU infusion pump reports repeated occlusion alarms.

Investigation:
- Tubing inspected: no blockage.
- Pressure sensor test: failed.
- Sensor replaced.
- Flow accuracy recalibrated.
- Electrical safety test passed.
- Device returned to service with maintenance record updated.
