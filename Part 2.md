# Infusion Pump Biomedical Engineering Presentation

## Part 2 – Slides 11–20

### Slide 11 – Electronic Control System
**Content**
- Microcontroller (CPU) is the brain of the infusion pump.
- Reads sensors continuously.
- Calculates motor pulses.
- Controls alarms, display, memory, and communication.

**Speaker Notes**
Explain that firmware performs real-time monitoring and safety checks.

---

### Slide 12 – Sensors
**Sensors**
- Air-in-line sensor
- Occlusion (pressure) sensor
- Door sensor
- Battery sensor
- Motor encoder

| Sensor | Purpose | Failure Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Air-in-line | Detects bubbles | Risk of air embolism |
| Occlusion | Detects blockage | Wrong infusion |
| Door | Ensures pump door is closed | Pump stops |
| Battery | Monitors battery | Unexpected shutdown |

---

### Slide 13 – Alarm System
Common alarms:
- Occlusion
- Air in line
- Low battery
- Door open
- End of infusion
- System error

Biomedical engineer should verify alarm functionality during preventive maintenance.

### Slide 15 – Acceptance Testing
Checklist:
- Visual inspection
- Verify accessories
- Power-on self-test
- Flow-rate accuracy
- Alarm verification
- Battery test
- Electrical safety

---

### Slide 16 – Preventive Maintenance
Daily:
- Clean exterior
- Check display
- Verify alarms

Every 6–12 months:
- Flow accuracy verification
- Battery capacity test
- Inspect tubing mechanism
- Electrical safety testing
- Firmware review (if applicable)

---

### Slide 17 – Cleaning and Disinfection
- Disconnect from AC power.
- Follow manufacturer-approved disinfectants.
- Never immerse the device.
- Prevent liquid from entering connectors.

---

### Slide 18 – Common Failure Modes
| Problem | Possible Cause | Corrective Action |
|---|---|---|
| No power | Blown fuse, failed PSU | Check power supply/fuse |
| Wrong flow | Calibration drift | Recalibrate |
| Battery alarm | Worn battery | Replace battery |
| Occlusion alarm | Blocked tubing | Inspect tubing and sensor |
| Air alarm | Air bubbles/sensor contamination | Prime line and clean sensor |
